How I Use AI

I use AI to enhance my workflow, not replace my skills. Alongside building AI-powered applications, I leverage tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to accelerate research, development, debugging, content creation, and iteration.

AI helps me work more efficiently, iterate faster, and increase productivity, but the strategy, design, development, and final execution are my own. I use AI where it adds value while ensuring the core thinking, decision-making, and majority of the work behind every project comes from me.

Result

A fully functional, solo-built gallery Q&A application that I can confidently explain from end to end—demonstrating my ability to integrate AI models into real-world products, troubleshoot issues, and deliver a working solution.

I continue to apply these skills on my portfolio, including BrieBot, my AI-powered resume assistant, which showcases how I design and integrate AI into user experiences.
